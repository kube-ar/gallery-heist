I started work at about a quarter to six.

Just after eight I went to the Dining Hall. I talked to Zeno Garrido there. At 21:30 I went to the Grand Atrium. At 22:00 I went out to the Terrace. I talked to Damian Hayashi there.

At 22:50 I was in the Grand Atrium. Odile Tamra was there too. I called Rocío around 20:15. I called Rocío at 21:35.

Pavel Lucero told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
