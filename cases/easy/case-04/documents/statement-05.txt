I started work around 18:00.

Around 20:00 I was in the Dining Hall. I talked to Ada Jessop there. At about half past nine I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to Dmitri there.

At about a quarter to eleven I was in the Dining Hall the whole time. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries.

Dmitri is my friend. Aurel told me money had been tight lately.

I didn't notice anything unusual.
