I started work at 17:25.

At about a quarter past seven I went to the Textile Gallery. At about half past seven I went to the Archive. At about a quarter past eight I went to the Restoration Lab. Around 9:00 I was in the Glass Room.

At about eleven I was on my round in the West Corridor. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries. Adrian Szabo called me at 20:20.

Adrian told me money had been tight lately.

I only heard about the painting when the guards closed the doors.
