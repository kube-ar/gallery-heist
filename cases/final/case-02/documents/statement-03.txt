I started work at about six.

At 19:25 I was in the Kitchen. I saw Freya Varga there. At 19:50 I was on the Terrace. I saw Sora there. At 20:15 I went to the Kitchen. I saw Mei there. At about half past nine I went to the Kitchen. I saw Sora there. At 22:10 I went out to the Terrace.

At 22:30 I was in the Dining Hall the whole time. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries.



I only heard about the painting when the guards closed the doors.
