I started work around 18:30. Around 7:25 I took a few guests through the Portrait Gallery.

At 19:25 I was in the Portrait Gallery. I saw Ines there. At 19:45 I went to the Grand Atrium. I talked to Odile Ferreira there. Around 20:00 I was in the Dining Hall. I talked to Teo Okafor there. At about half past nine I went to the Grand Atrium. I saw Nico Hayashi there.

Around 21:35 I was in the Grand Atrium. Bram was there too.

Caio Esposito told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
