I started work at about half past five.

Around 7:35 I went to the Lantern Gallery. At about half past seven I was in the Archive. Around 20:30 I went to the Portrait Gallery.

At about a quarter to eleven I was on my round in the East Corridor. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries. I called Aurelio Balogun around 19:00.

Alba told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
