I started work at 18:00. Around 19:25 I took a few guests through the Map Room.

At about half past seven I was in the Map Room. I saw Ada Olander there. At 19:45 I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to Camille there. Around 8:00 I was in the Dining Hall. I saw one of the waiters there. At about a quarter past ten I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to Kasimir Grünwald there.

At about half past ten I was on the Terrace the whole time. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries.

Aurelio Varga is my friend. Joaquín is my friend. Selim told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
