I started work at 17:30.

At about half past seven I was in the Map Room. At 19:35 I was in the Restoration Lab. At 21:25 I went to the Restoration Lab.

At about half past ten I was on my round in the East Corridor. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries. I called Zeno Garrido around 6:20.

Dev told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
