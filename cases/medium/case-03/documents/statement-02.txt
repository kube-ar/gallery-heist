I arrived around 7:40.

At about a quarter to eight I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to one of the donors there. Just after nine I went to the Dining Hall. I talked to Nico Rinholt there. At about half past nine I went to the Grand Atrium. I talked to Zofia Thonre there.

At 22:35 I was in the Dining Hall the whole time. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries.



The music was loud and the rooms were full.
