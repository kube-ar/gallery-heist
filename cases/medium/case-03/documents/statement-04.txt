I started work at 18:25.

Just after eight I went to the Dining Hall. I talked to Rhea there. At about half past nine I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to Yusuf Kminski there.

Around 10:00 I was on the Terrace. Lars Orsini was there too.

Dev told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
