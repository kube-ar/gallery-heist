I started work around 17:20.

At about a quarter to eight I went to the Archive. At about a quarter to nine I went to the Security Office. I saw Camille there. At 21:20 I went to the Sculpture Hall.

At 23:00 I was on my round in the West Corridor. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries.

Tobias is my friend. Maelle Nakagawa is my friend. Marek Kaminski told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
