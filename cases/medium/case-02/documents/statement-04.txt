I started work just after six.

At about eight I went to the Dining Hall. I talked to Rocío Garrido there. Around 8:20 I was in the Dining Hall. At about half past nine I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to Mila Castaño there. Around 10:15 I went to the Dining Hall.

At 23:00 I was in the Grand Atrium. one of the waiters was there too.



The music was loud and the rooms were full.
