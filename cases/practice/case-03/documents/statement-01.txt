I started work at about half past five.

At about a quarter past seven I was in the Archive. At 19:20 I was in the Security Office. I saw Sora there. Around 20:45 I went to the Sculpture Hall. Around 20:50 I went to the Print Room. At 20:55 I went to the Archive.

At 22:45 I was in the Security Office. Sora was there too.

Adrian is my friend. Emeka Whitlock told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
