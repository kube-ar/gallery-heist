I started work at 17:25.

At about a quarter past seven I went to the Tapestry Hall. Around 19:25 I went to the Miniatures Room. Around 20:15 I was in the Archive. Just after nine I went to the Tapestry Hall. Just after nine I was in the Restoration Lab.

At about a quarter past ten I was on my round in the East Corridor. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries. Nadia Jessop called me at 20:15.

Zeno told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
