I started work at 18:05. At about half past seven I took a few guests through the Lantern Gallery.

At about half past seven I was in the Lantern Gallery. I saw Ezra there. At 19:45 I was in the Grand Atrium. Around 8:00 I was in the Dining Hall. I talked to Orla Rossetti there. At 21:30 I went to the Grand Atrium. I saw Vera Strand there.

At 22:20 I was in the Grand Atrium. Mara was there too. Mara called me around 9:45.

Viggo Garrido is my friend.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
