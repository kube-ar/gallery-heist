I arrived around 7:25.

Around 7:30 I went to the Grand Atrium. I talked to Mara Ibarra there. Around 8:00 I was in the Dining Hall. I saw Mila Dosrey there. At 21:30 I went to the Grand Atrium. I talked to an artist there.

At about a quarter to ten I was in the Grand Atrium. the registrar was there too.



The music was loud and the rooms were full.
