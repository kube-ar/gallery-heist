I started work at 17:30.

Around 20:00 I went to the Dining Hall. I saw Pavel Jessop there. At 21:30 I was in the Grand Atrium. I talked to Quentin Lafayette there. Just after ten I was on the Terrace. I talked to Saoirse there.

At about a quarter to eleven I was in the Grand Atrium. an artist was there too.

Saoirse told me money had been tight lately.

I didn't notice anything unusual.
