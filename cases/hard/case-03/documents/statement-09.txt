I started work at 17:55.

At about half past seven I went to the Kitchen. I saw Yusuf there. Around 7:45 I went to the Dining Hall. I talked to Jun Hollis there. At about a quarter past eight I went to the Kitchen. At 21:15 I went out to the Terrace.

At 22:50 I was in the Dining Hall the whole time. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
