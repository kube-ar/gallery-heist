I started work around 17:25.

At about half past seven I went to the Lantern Gallery. I saw Valentin Bellweather there. At 19:45 I was in the Archive. Around 22:30 I was in the Silver Room. At about half past ten I went to the Portrait Gallery. Around 22:40 I went to the Restoration Lab.

At about a quarter to eleven I was in the Security Office. Noor was there too. Zofia called me around 12:10.

Jun told me money had been tight lately.

The music was loud and the rooms were full.
