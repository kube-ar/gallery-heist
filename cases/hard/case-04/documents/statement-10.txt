I started work at about half past five.

Around 7:45 I was in the Archive. At about half past eight I went to the Silver Room. I saw Ximena there. At about a quarter to nine I was in the Map Room. At about a quarter to ten I was in the Security Office. I saw Jun Holt there.

At 22:55 I was on my round in the East Corridor. I didn't go anywhere near the galleries. I called Gid around 18:30.

Lars Aldana told me money had been tight lately.

I only heard about the painting when the guards closed the doors.
